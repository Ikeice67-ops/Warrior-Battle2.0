📜 Game Rules

Four champions. A 20×20 battlefield. Gather resources, raise buildings, upgrade your army, and be the last champion standing.

🎯 Goal

Eliminate every enemy unit. You lose when your last warrior falls — the last player with at least one unit alive wins.

🗺️ The Battlefield

  • 🌿 Field — open ground. The only place you can build.
  • 🌲 Forest — gather wood here.
  • 🧱 Brick tile — gather brick here.
  • ⛰️ Mountain — impassable until you own a pickaxe; gather ore once you can enter.

🕹️ Taking Your Turn

  • Click one of your warriors to select it, then click a highlighted 🟢 green tile to move — one square per click, including diagonals.
  • Each unit has limited movement steps per turn (shown in the stats readout). Moving onto a resource tile positions you to gather.
  • Press End Turn or hit Enter to pass to the next champion. Press Q/E to rotate the board view.

⚔️ Combat

  • Click a highlighted 🔴 red enemy to attack. Attacking uses that unit's action for the rest of the turn (it can't move, gather, or build afterwards) — but your turn continues with your other units.
  • Every unit has an attack value (damage dealt) and a counter value (damage returned).
  • A surviving defender counterattacks if its counter is above 0 and you're within its range. A counter of 0 means no retaliation.
  • Capture: melee units that finish off an enemy take their square, like a chess capture. Ranged kills (arrows, catapult stones) don't move the attacker.

🌲 Resources & Gathering

  • Start your turn standing on a resource tile and click the floating icon above your warrior to collect. Gathering ends that unit's movement for the turn, but not your turn.
  • Wood 🪵 — forests. Without an axe, collect 1 wood every 2 harvesting turns. A normal axe collects 1 wood per turn; a reinforced axe collects 2. Leaving the forest resets progress.
  • Brick 🧱 — brick tiles, one turn.
  • Ore ⛏️ — you need a pickaxe to enter mountains. A normal pickaxe collects 1 ore every 2 mining turns; a reinforced pickaxe collects 1 ore per turn. Leaving the mountain resets mining progress.
  • Resources and tools are shared across all of your units; each unit can gather once per turn.

🛠️ Crafting

  • Spend 4 wood to craft an 🪓 axe or ⛏️ pickaxe. Reinforce either tool for 2 wood + 3 ore: a reinforced axe gathers 2 wood per turn, while a reinforced pickaxe gathers 1 ore per turn. Crafting or reinforcing ends your turn.

⛺ Camps, Villages & Fortresses

  • Camp: stand on a field with 1 wood + 1 brick, click the floating ⛺ icon. Building ends your turn.
  • Village: upgrade your camp with 2 wood + 2 brick + 1 ore. Ends your turn.
  • Fortress: upgrade your village with 3 wood + 3 brick + 3 ore. Ends your turn.
  • Claiming enemy buildings: move a unit onto an enemy camp, town, or fortress and keep that same unit there until your next turn begins. Select the unit and click the floating 🚩 flag to claim the structure for your team. Leaving the tile or being defeated before then resets the claim. Claiming uses up that unit's actions for the turn.
  • Summoning: deselect your units, then click an empty building you own. Camps summon a Warrior (1 ore) only while you have zero Warriors. Villages allow up to 2 Warriors, plus one each of Knight/Guard/Archer (2 ore). Fortresses allow up to 3 Warriors, two each of Knight/Guard/Archer, and one each of Paladin/Catapult/Swordsman (3 ore). Freshly summoned units can't move until your next turn.

⬆️ Upgrading Units

  • Select a unit and click the floating ⬆️ icon: Warriors become Knights, Archers, or Guards for 2 ore; Knights → Paladins, Archers → Catapults, Guards → Swordsmen for 3 ore.
  • Class limits match the fortress summoning limits. Current HP carries over (up to the new class maximum), and the upgraded unit can't act again this turn.

📌 Key Rules to Remember

  • Attacking does not end your turn — only that unit's actions. Your other units can still move, gather, and build.
  • You may attack before using all of a unit's movement steps.
  • Each unit may gather once per turn, and cannot move after gathering (other units still can).
  • Mountains block diagonal corner-cutting: you can't slip between two adjacent mountains without a pickaxe.
  • Leaving a forest before finishing the two-turn wood harvest resets your progress.
  • Resources and tools are shared across all of your units — but each unit tracks its own HP and actions.
  • The building you summon from must be unoccupied, and summoned units can't move until your next turn.
  • Only melee kills capture the enemy's square — ranged kills (archer, catapult) leave the attacker in place.
  • Champions with no living units are skipped automatically until only one remains.
